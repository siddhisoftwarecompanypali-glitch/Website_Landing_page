— Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Clear answers about timelines, tech stacks, budgets, and how we collaborate with you.

We specialize in full-cycle digital product development: bespoke mobile applications (iOS & Android using Flutter and React Native), modern responsive web platforms, scalable backend architecture, interactive 2D/3D games (Unity), and custom AI & workflow automation integrations.

Timelines depend on project complexity. An MVP (Minimum Viable Product) or custom business website usually takes 3 to 6 weeks. Comprehensive multi-platform applications and enterprise software suites typically take between 8 to 16 weeks. We work in 2-week agile sprints with continuous progress demos.

Yes. We build practical, business-ready AI solutions — from intelligent document parsing and custom chatbot/LLM agents to predictive analytics, recommendation algorithms, and computer vision systems tailored to your specific workflows.

We believe in transparent, honest pricing. We offer Fixed-Scope Contracts for well-defined deliverables (MVPs typically start from $1,000 to $5,000) and Dedicated Sprint Retainers for long-term product engineering. We provide an itemized proposal with clear milestones before starting.

Every project we launch comes with an initial post-launch warranty period to ensure seamless adoption. Afterward, we offer flexible monthly maintenance packages covering 99.9% uptime monitoring, server security patches, iOS/Android version compatibility, and feature enhancements.

Our engineering headquarters is based in Pali, Rajasthan, India. We work with clients across India, North America, Europe, and the Middle East, offering dedicated sprint managers, transparent Slack/WhatsApp communication, and scheduled weekly reviews.
